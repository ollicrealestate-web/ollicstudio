How it works

Three steps from image to film.

Claude AI reads your image and your words, writes the prompts and drives the best image and video AI for each job.

01

Upload

A PNG or JPEG: a sketch, a Revit, SketchUp or Lumion view, a render, a photo or a floor plan picture. Stored privately in your project.

02

Describe

Say what you want in plain words. The AI studies the image and writes the prompt; you can read and edit it before anything is made.

03

Get the result

Renders, animations and video clips are made in the background and saved to your project's Library.

What you get

One image. Every output matches it.

The golden animation

Your 3D image becomes a photoreal night view. The building's lines are drawn in glowing gold from the ground up, then a line of light sweeps up and reveals the finished building. Up to three images make one video.

Photoreal renders

A sketch or 3D view becomes a photoreal picture of the same building, from the same camera.

Floor plan to 3D

A picture of a floor plan becomes photoreal 3D views that follow its layout.

Cinematic clips

One continuous AI video shot: the gold wireframe turns into the real building while the camera moves.

Revise in plain words

"Make the roof dark grey." Each change is a new version; the original stays.

Ready for WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok

MP4 videos with titles and your branding, in the shape each platform wants.
