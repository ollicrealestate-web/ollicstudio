How it works

Three steps from drawing to film.

Nothing is guessed. Ollic reads your file, shows you what it found, and builds only from what you confirm.

01

Upload

A PNG or JPEG sketch, a PDF drawing, or an IFC exported from Revit or ArchiCAD. Each file is checked from its contents and stored privately.

02

Confirm

Check the walls, openings and dimensions. Missing values are flagged for you to fill in, never invented.

03

Watch it build

Renders, the construction film and the walkthrough are produced in the background and saved to your project.

What you get

One model. Every output matches it.

The construction film

Every stage of a Nigerian residential build, from setting out and excavation through sandcrete blockwork, long-span roofing, MEP first fix and finishes, to furnished rooms with the lights on. Inspection hold points are marked along the way.

Model renders

Exterior and interior views from your model, so walls and windows sit where the plan has them.

MEP view

Water, drainage, electrical and AC runs, one system at a time, with the walls ghosted.

Plan tour

A person walks your floor plan through its real doors, and each room is revealed with its name and area.

Top-down reveal

Your line plan wipes into a rendered, furnished overhead view, aligned wall for wall.
