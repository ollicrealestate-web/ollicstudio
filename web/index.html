Four ways to use the studio

Each one starts from something you already have: your model, a drawing or an image. Nothing to install.

New

From your model: renders and AI walkthroughs

Upload your Revit (.rvt) or IFC model and move around it in 3D. Ollic records the exact view or camera move from your model, the AI makes it photoreal, and every result is checked against your model's lines.

  • Claude plans the walkthrough shots: approach, orbit, aerial, rise and slow move in.
  • Add life: people walking and cars passing.
  • Each shot is ready in minutes, not hours of rendering.
The brief that made this renderModern two-storey luxury villa in Lekki, Lagos: white render walls, large bronze-framed glass windows, flat roof with cantilevered upper floor, palm trees and interlocking paving.

Photoreal render

Start from a sketch, a 3D view or a photo of a room or a facade. The picture you choose keeps its shape and camera; up to three reference images set the scene, the materials and the light.

  • The AI studies your picture and writes the prompt; you can edit it before anything is made.
  • Exteriors and interiors: facades, lobbies, kitchens, bedrooms.
  • Change anything afterwards in plain words, like “make the roof dark grey”.

Floor plan to 3D

Upload a picture of a floor plan. Ollic reads the rooms and the layout, then makes exterior and interior views that follow it, ready for an early client meeting.

  • Room names, areas and the overall shape are read for you.
  • Add a reference photo to set the style of the finished house.
Video

Golden animation

Turn up to three 3D images into a short film. The building is drawn in glowing gold lines from the ground up, then a line of light sweeps up and reveals the photoreal render.

  • Landscape, portrait and square, for presentations, Reels, Status and TikTok.
  • Or make one continuous AI camera move from the gold lines to the finished building.

How a render is made

About a minute from upload to a picture you can send; a few minutes for a walkthrough shot.

  1. Upload your model or image

    A Revit or IFC model, a sketch, a 3D view, a photo or a floor plan (PDF, PNG or JPEG).

  2. Add references

    Optional: up to three photos whose setting, materials and mood you like.

  3. Let the AI write the prompt

    Press “Read image and write prompt”, then read and edit it, or describe the building yourself.

  4. Render and refine

    Choose the AI that suits the job, render, then revise in plain words. Every version is kept.
